## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an institutional yet deeply welcoming, human-centric visual identity for a social youth organization. It bridges the authority and transparency necessary for institutional donors, corporate partners, and public audits with the energetic, warm, and hopeful spirit of youth development and community care.

### Design Tone & Movements
- **Warm Institutionalism:** A fusion of modern clean corporate reliability and accessible non-profit warmth. Surfaces are clear, generous, and bright, avoiding cold clinical corporate austerity.
- **Vibrant Optimism:** Uplifting accents, warm tonal gradients, friendly corner radii, and tactile elevation invite interaction, engagement, and voluntary donation.
- **Accessibility & Clarity:** High-contrast reading hierarchies and clear calls-to-action (CTAs) ensure users of all digital literacies—from local community members to executive donors—navigate initiatives, transparency reports, and donation flows effortlessly.

## Colors

The color system combines institutional trust, community vitality, and welcoming warmth:

- **Deep Navy (`#1E3A8A`):** The primary anchor. Communicates permanence, financial integrity, and institutional trust. Used for dominant headings, critical structural cards, and high-level navigation.
- **Vibrant Blue (`#2563EB`):** The secondary driver. Channels youth momentum and active engagement. Utilized for interactive links, primary action states, and key navigational highlights.
- **Hope Green (`#10B981`):** The tertiary social impact tone. Symbolizes growth, vitality, transformation, and completed goals. Reserved for success badges, progress meters, social outcome metrics, and impact affirmations.
- **Warm Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`):** The catalytic accent. Evokes juvenile energy, optimism, and warmth. Dedicated to direct conversion mechanisms: donation triggers, instant Pix support cards, urgent volunteering banners, and attention badges.
- **Neutral Slate (`#475569`):** Grounded neutrals ensuring legibility and natural contrast against warm tinted backgrounds (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`). Avoid pure `#000000` text to maintain approachable softness.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate sense of welcoming professionalism:

- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Its organic geometric curves, generous x-height, and optimistic counterforms eliminate sterility while maintaining solid structural clarity. Bold weights deliver decisive, hopeful calls to action.
- **Body & Continuous Text (`Inter`):** Engineered for exceptional digital legibility. It handles dense institutional reports, testimonials, mission outlines, and educational agendas with maximum clarity across devices.
- **Labels & Microcopy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Employs slightly boosted tracking and medium-to-bold weights for badges, Pix keys, metric indicators, and navigation links.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid responsive grid anchored by an 8pt spatial cadence:

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile`: `< 640px` (4 columns, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters)
  - `Tablet`: `640px – 1024px` (8 columns, `1.5rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters)
  - `Desktop`: `> 1024px` (12 columns, max-width `1280px` centered, `2rem` gutters)
- **Rhythm & Padding:**
  - Section blocks utilize generous vertical intervals (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) on desktop to avoid cramped, institutional bureaucracy.
  - Interactive cards maintain generous inner clearance (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to promote readability and visual warmth.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft ambient diffusion tinted with oceanic undertones, steering clear of stark, muddy gray shadows:

- **Base Surface (`Level 0`):** `#FFFFFF` or pale canvas `#F8FAFC` with subtle borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Card Ambient (`Level 1`):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.03)`. Gives cards a gentle lift while remaining calm and approachable.
- **Interactive / Hover Surface (`Level 2`):** `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.10), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.04)`. Elevates hovered cards, Pix copy containers, and dropdowns.
- **Modal / Floating CTA (`Level 3`):** `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.18)`. Applied to fixed donation bars, donation modals, and urgent alert drawers.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses warmth, safety, and modern community design:

- **Standard Radii (`rounded-xl` / 16px):** Applied to content cards, input boxes, informational banners, and list tiles.
- **Large Radii (`rounded-2xl` / 24px):** Applied to hero blocks, donation highlight cards, modal sheets, and featured project containers.
- **Pill Radii (`rounded-full`):** Applied to status badges, category pills, avatar wrappers, and prominent round CTAs (such as fast-action Pix buttons).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional (`#2563EB`):** High-saturation blue with white text, `rounded-xl` or `rounded-full`, bold typography (`label-lg`), subtle lift on hover.
- **Donation & Action (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`):** Warm amber gradient or solid amber with deep contrast navy text (`#1E3A8A`) or white text. Used exclusively for financial contributions, Pix keys, and direct social calls.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1.5px` border in `#1E3A8A` or subtle `#E2E8F0` with slate text.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Compact pills (`rounded-full`) with light tonal backgrounds and saturated text:
  - **Social Impact / Health:** Green background (`#ECFDF5`), green text (`#047857`).
  - **Youth & Culture:** Amber background (`#FEF3C7`), amber text (`#B45309`).
  - **Institutional / Transparency:** Light blue background (`#EFF6FF`), navy text (`#1E3A8A`).

### Cards & Project Highlights
- Rendered with `rounded-2xl`, `#FFFFFF` surface, ambient Level 1 elevation, and a crisp `1px solid #F1F5F9` edge.
- Visual hierarchy: High-quality workshop/youth photography with softened upper corners, followed by a category badge, clear `headline-sm`, summary text, and a direct action link.

### Donation & Pix Module
- High-priority surface with warm dual-tone background (soft amber-to-white or deep institutional navy with amber accents).
- Integrated one-click Pix copy field with immediate feedback toast. Preset value chips (e.g., R$ 20, R$ 50, R$ 100, Outro) with clear selected states.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** `rounded-xl` with `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, smooth focus transition to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `rounded-md` or circular, adopting `#10B981` or `#2563EB` check fills with smooth micro-transitions.

### Impact Counters & Metrics
- Prominent statistical callouts pairing `display-hero` metrics in `#1E3A8A` with sub-labels in `#10B981` and `#475569`, communicating verifiable community impact with clarity and dignity.
